MYCO — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

The short version

MYCO collects nothing. No data ever leaves your device.

There is no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising network, no crash reporting,

no tracking, and no server. The game is a single web page that runs entirely in your browser.

What is stored, and where

MYCO saves your game progress in your own browser, using the browser's localStorage.

This never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. It contains:

  • puzzle progress, stars and streaks
  • in-game currency balances (Spores, Glowcaps) and power-up counts
  • daily-reward and quest state
  • a randomly generated device identifier used only for the in-game invite feature

That identifier is a random string generated on your device. It is not linked to you, your

name, your email, or any other identity. We never see it.

To erase everything: clear your browser's site data for this page. That deletes all of it

permanently. There is no copy anywhere else, because no copy was ever sent anywhere.

Network requests

MYCO makes no network requests of any kind after the page loads. It contains no third-party

fonts, no external images, no audio files, no scripts from other domains, no CDN calls, and no

analytics beacons. All artwork is drawn as inline vector graphics and all sound is generated

mathematically in your browser. The game works fully offline.

Third parties

We use none. There are no advertising SDKs, no analytics providers, and no embedded services.

If you play MYCO on a games portal (for example CrazyGames, Poki or itch.io), **that portal's

own privacy policy applies to their page and their advertising**, independently of this game.

We do not receive any data from them about you.

Purchases

There are no purchases. The in-game store spends only in-game currency that you earn by playing.

Items shown with a real-world price are not active, take no payment, and grant nothing.

No payment information is requested, processed, or stored.

Children

MYCO contains no data collection, no chat, no user-generated content, no social features, no

advertising, and no purchases. It is suitable for all ages.

Your rights

Because we never receive any of your data, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export,

or delete on your behalf. You hold the only copy, in your browser, and you can delete it at any

time by clearing site data.
